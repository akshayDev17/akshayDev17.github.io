7 · Bullets are marked by a 2px left border, never a dash or a bullet glyph.
8 · Role dates are hidden when they equal the company range — no repeated "Jul 2025 — Present" on both lines.
9 · Below 1024px everything stacks: rail on top, chips drop to the bottom of the card.

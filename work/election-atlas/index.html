All 543 seats, grouped by the party that won them and split by state

Real
State and union territory boundaries, the list of regions, and how many seats each returns — Uttar Pradesh 80, Maharashtra 48, and so on, 543 in all.
Not real
Which party won anything. The party-level seat splits, and every vote and elector figure, are placeholders. They are internally consistent, but they are not the published result — so the map's colours and every share on this page are illustrative, not fact.
Derived, not stored
Result files hold absolute counts only. Every percentage, every leading party and the position of the majority line are computed in the browser as the page loads.
Boundaries
The geometry predates the 2019 reorganisation: Ladakh has no polygon, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu are still separate regions. A post-2019 source is needed before publication.
